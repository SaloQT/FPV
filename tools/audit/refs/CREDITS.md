`sources.json` (file, title, licence, source page, author as Commons gives it). Every photo here is CC0, CC BY, CC BY-SA or public
domain, so it may be redistributed; the CC BY and CC BY-SA ones require the credit below to stay with them. The licence is the one
Commons listed when the photo was fetched (2026-10-10); the source page is authoritative. pine-branch/8-11 are pen drawings, not
photos. spruce-tree/7.jpg (Wolfgang Moroder, "Pëc de Poz Urtijëi Picea abies", CC BY-SA 3.0) was left out: its author adds terms on top
of the licence, so it stays only in the untracked .bench/audit/refs.
